Stillness reveals the Self

Spirituality is not a religious act.
It is the way to know the Self.

Spirituality is not running away from life.
It is aligning with life.

Spirituality

Spirituality is not belief, ritual, or escape.
It is the direct knowing of the Self —
awareness recognizing itself.

Life continues. Inner conflict ends.

Mind · Body · Soul

Body — the gateway to the present.

Mind — thoughts and emotions to be observed.

Soul — awareness itself, the witness.

The body is experienced. The mind is observed. The soul is what observes.

Yoga

Yoga is not fitness. Yoga is alignment.

Karma yoga

Alignment through action

Bhakti yoga

Alignment through devotion

Jnana yoga

Alignment through knowing

Raja yoga

Alignment through stillness

Meditation

Meditation is not effort.
It is allowing.

When the body rests and the mind quiets,
awareness remains.

The One Asana

Choose any one asana. Let the body be still.
Breathe consciously. Do not try to connect —
allow connection to happen.

Every musical instrument is tuned before it is played. In the same way, tune yourself for a better melody of life.
